About the Website

Introduction

The Student Portal is a simple website where students can submit their details and view saved student records.

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to make collecting and viewing student information easy and organised.

Mission

Our mission is to provide a clear, simple and reliable system for managing student information.

Services / Features

  • Student information form with validation
  • Records saved safely in a MongoDB database
  • Student records page showing all saved students
  • Simple and clean dashboard
